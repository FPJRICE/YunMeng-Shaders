YunMeng Shaders - 0.1.0 Alpha 7

Added
- Custom End sky with flowing gold-trimmed silk-scroll waves and centered pale-gold brush calligraphy in Wuxia Smooth, plus 3D wave columns with enchanting-style runes in Minecraft Voxel.
- Configurable End void glyphs (Classical Chinese, Brush/Pixel SGA, or Off), varied-angle End starfield, and atmospheric End depth fog.
- Updated Guofeng Moon artwork with eight lunar phases and atmospheric blending.
- Glowing mob eyes and emissive markings support with configurable eye-glow bloom.
- Full-resolution night star reflections on water and ice behind clouds and terrain.
- Procedural rain rework with ground/water impact ripples, harmonized splash particles, and distant rain-curtain mist.

Changed
- Harmonized Sun, Moon, cloud, and light-shaft blending across all biome skyboxes and weather states.
- Darkened End block and terrain lighting to match the misty End atmosphere.
- Categorized foliage sway by attachment type so surface clingers stay pinned, wall vines never clip into blocks, and hanging strands sway from their tips.

Fixed
- Fixed dithered glowing speckles on cherry tree water reflections at night.
- Improved Xianxia Mist underwater surface blending and distant land outline visibility.
- Fixed third-person dynamic held-light flickering.
